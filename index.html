OUR STORY

Roasted on-site, served with jazz

A relative newcomer to Ljubljana's coffee scene, R & B roasts its own beans right on the premises — rare Colombian lots alongside a rotating single-origin selection. The old-school jazz-club atmosphere pairs surprisingly well with a proper brunch menu: American pancakes, avocado toast, and a French press pour worth lingering over. Luggage storage and a roasting class round out the reasons regulars keep coming back.
